## Known limitations (chosen not to fix)

1. **Fee revenue: two sources disagree.** 9,350 of 10,396 client-years of Advisory Fee debits in `fact_transactions` differ from `fact_fees` by >10%. *Reason:* `fact_fees` reconciles 100% to `aum_basis x bps / 10,000 / 12` and to snapshot AUM, so it is the system of record; forcing the debits to match would invent data.
2. **Dividend `amount` = full position value** (53,826 rows). *Reason:* consistent source definition; the real dividend cannot be recovered. Do not sum it as income.
3. **No market holidays in `dim_date`** (9 weekday Jan 1 / Jul 4 / Dec 25 dates are trading days). *Reason:* a partial holiday fix would be misleading; `non_trading_day_flag` therefore only catches weekends.
4. **Floor AUM and extreme returns are flagged, not changed.** *Reason:* the true values cannot be recovered; filter on `aum_floor_flag` / `return_outlier_flag` / `alpha_outlier_flag`.
5. **Trades on weekends and post-close transactions are flagged, not moved or deleted.** *Reason:* the true dates cannot be recovered and deleting would distort volumes.
6. **Imputed risk profiles** are best-evidence, not confirmed by the client. *Reason:* filled only where the assigned benchmark maps to exactly one profile; `risk_profile_imputed_flag` marks them.
7. **Snapshot months without a fee row** (7,350). *Reason:* waivers or billing gaps are plausible and there is nothing to fill from.
8. **Legitimate blanks kept**: ETF `sector` (219), stock `expense_ratio_bps` (281), cash-flow `security_id`/`shares`/`price` (101,528). *Reason:* the value does not exist for those rows; filling would invent data.
9. **Unused dimension members** (benchmark_id 6, dates with no activity). *Reason:* reference data, normal in a star schema.
10. **Same-name clients are not merged** (e.g. Deborah Miller, 1626 / 2060). *Reason:* different city, ZIP, advisor and history - two different people.
